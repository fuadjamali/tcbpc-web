What changes in the app if you approve

  • Cut-out photos (v3): AI background removal (rembg) on all four Dojo Go photos — including the white finishes — then 3× Lanczos upscale, white-halo removal, sharpening and a little contrast/colour. Saved as transparent WebP (~40–75 KB each) — added under public/images/tcbpc/ and used as the product photos, so the hero stage, flagship preview and product page all use them. Product cards on white look the same as before.
  • Bigger, pops out of the card: device ~470px tall, overflowing the top of the stage card instead of sitting inside a white box.
  • 3D + lively: idle float, mouse/scroll tilt with a moving shadow and glow, and a 3D swing transition between photos.
  • Premium finish: thin teal rim light, layered studio shadow, a soft spotlight behind the device, a faint floor reflection, and a glossy light sweep that runs across the device itself (clipped to its silhouette) in time with the 3-second change.
  • Auto-advance every 3 s through the product's photos (the finish chips highlight and stay clickable, progress bars show timing). Pauses on hover and for visitors who prefer reduced motion. Images only — nothing else on the page moves.
  • Still admin-driven: photos, labels and captions come from the product's photo manager; no new database fields. The cut-outs replace the photo files for those two products.
  • Limit to know: the supplied photos are only 640px wide, so the device is upscaled — it looks good at this size but will soften if made much larger. Higher-resolution photos from Dojo would be sharper; swapping them later is just an upload.
